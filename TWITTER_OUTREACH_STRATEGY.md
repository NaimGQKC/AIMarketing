### Thread 2: "Why Your Brand is Invisible to AI" (Technical Deep-Dive)

```
1/ Why your brand is invisible to AI - and what the fix actually looks like.

A technical deep-dive for marketers who want to understand the machine, 
not just the symptoms.

2/ First, let's understand HOW AI decides to recommend your brand.

It's not like Google. There's no "ranking." The LLM is REASONING 
through a chain of logic to construct an answer.

Your brand either fits into that chain - or it doesn't exist.

3/ The AI's reasoning chain looks like this:

User asks: "Best [product] for [need]"
AI retrieves: Context from training data + live web search
AI reasons: "Which brands match this need with high confidence?"
AI outputs: A recommendation (or doesn't)

4/ YOUR BRAND FAILS AT STEP 2 AND 3.

Step 2 failure: The AI can't find fresh, structured data about your brand.
It finds a 2021 blog post, a Reddit thread, maybe a cached product page.

Step 3 failure: Even when it finds your data, it can't REASON about it 
with confidence.

5/ Why can't it reason with confidence?

Because your product data is:
- Unstructured (paragraph text, not schema)
- Incomplete (no pricing, no specs in machine-readable format)
- Monolingual (English only, or French only)
- Stale (last updated 8 months ago)

6/ THE FIX IS NOT "WRITE MORE CONTENT."

I repeat: this is NOT a content problem. You can publish 500 blog posts 
and still be invisible to ChatGPT.

The fix is STRUCTURAL. You need to speak the AI's language.

7/ FIX #1: Bilingual JSON-LD Schema

JSON-LD is a structured data format that sits in your page's <head>.

It tells the AI: "This brand sells X product, at Y price, in Z categories, 
with these specifications, in English AND French."

The AI doesn't have to guess. You DECLARE it.

8/ FIX #2: Entity Optimization

The AI needs to understand your brand as an ENTITY - not just a collection 
of web pages.

- Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across all directories
- Wikipedia/Wikidata presence
- Knowledge Graph eligibility signals

(Shoutout to @jasonmbarnard who's been preaching this for years)

9/ FIX #3: Agentic Probing - Verify the Fix Works

This is the part nobody talks about.

After deploying fixes, you need to PROBE the AI:
- Run 50+ queries across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity
- In both languages
- Track recommendation shifts over time

We call this "Headless Agentic Probing."

10/ FIX #4: Feed the AI Directly

Google's UCP (Universal Content Protocol) and MCP-compatible structured feeds (MCP is the dominant standard — 97M+ monthly SDK downloads, governed by Linux Foundation Agentic AI Foundation) let you push structured data directly to AI systems.

Instead of waiting for crawlers, you PUSH your product catalog in a format 
the AI can immediately reason from.

11/ We built this entire workflow into @VisiMind:

Connect (sync PIM data) -> Diagnose (find signal gaps) -> Remediate 
(deploy fix-kits) -> Verify (prove the AI changed its mind)

It's not magic. It's plumbing. But it's plumbing that most brands 
don't know they need.

12/ If you want to check your brand's AI visibility right now, 
here's a free 2-minute test:

1. Open ChatGPT
2. Ask: "What are the best [your category] brands?"
3. Ask the same question in French (or your second language)
4. Compare the results

If you're not there - now you know why. DM me for details.
```

## PART 4: REPLY STRATEGY (10 Templates)

Use these when engaging in conversations about AI search, GEO, brand visibility. The goal: be helpful, build credibility, never pitch directly.

### Reply 1: When someone asks "How do I show up in ChatGPT?"
```
The biggest lever is structured data - specifically JSON-LD schema with Product, 
Organization, and FAQPage types. ChatGPT can go from 16% to 54% accuracy on 
your brand when it has clean schema to reason from. Most brands are still relying 
on unstructured page text, which is why they're invisible.
```

### Reply 2: When someone shares data about AI search trends
```
This tracks with what we're seeing too. One thing that's underreported: the gap 
is even wider for non-English queries. We tested French vs English for the same 
brand catalogs and saw a 60% drop in recommendations. Token fragmentation in 
non-English languages is a massive blind spot.
```

### Reply 3: When someone complains about AI recommending competitors
```
This is usually a structured data problem, not a brand authority problem. 
The AI recommends whoever it can reason about with the highest confidence. 
If your competitor has clean schema and you have paragraph text - they win, 
regardless of who's actually better. Worth auditing your JSON-LD as step one.
```

### Reply 4: When a GEO expert shares a new framework
```
Really useful framework. One dimension I'd add: language parity testing. 
We've found that most GEO strategies only test in English, but the same 
brand can have wildly different AI visibility scores across languages - 
even on the same platform. Multilingual probing should be part of every 
GEO audit.
```

### Reply 5: When someone asks about the difference between SEO and GEO
```
The simplest way to think about it: SEO optimizes for RANKING (position on 
a list). GEO optimizes for REASONING (being included in an AI's logic chain). 
You can rank #1 on Google and be completely invisible to ChatGPT if the AI 
can't parse your data into a confident recommendation.
```

### Reply 6: When someone discusses structured data / schema markup
```
JSON-LD is becoming non-negotiable for AI visibility. The key insight most 
people miss: it's not just about HAVING schema - it's about having it in 
EVERY language your customers use. A bilingual schema can 2-3x your AI 
recommendation rate in non-English markets.
```

### Reply 7: When someone shares a "ChatGPT recommended X instead of my brand" screenshot
```
This is more common than people realize. The AI falls back to whatever data 
it has highest confidence in - which is often a 3-year-old Reddit thread or 
a cached blog post. The fix: give the AI fresher, more structured data about 
your brand so it doesn't have to guess. JSON-LD + entity optimization is the 
starting point.
```

### Reply 8: When someone discusses Canadian / Quebec digital marketing
```
One thing that's underexplored in Canadian digital: how badly AI search 
handles bilingual content. LLMs tokenize French technical terms into 3-5x 
more fragments than English. For any brand serving Quebec, this means your 
French-speaking customers are getting significantly worse AI recommendations. 
Worth testing with a simple ChatGPT query in both languages.
```

### Reply 9: When someone asks "Is GEO worth investing in yet?"
```
Depends on your market, but consider this: 28% of ChatGPT's most-cited pages 
have zero organic Google visibility (Ahrefs data). That means AI search is 
already creating a parallel discovery layer that doesn't follow traditional SEO. 
If your competitors figure this out first, you're playing catch-up on a channel 
that's growing 40%+ YoY.
```

### Reply 10: When an e-commerce brand asks about AI-proofing their store

2. Ask ChatGPT "Best [your category] brands" and see if you appear
3. If you serve multilingual markets, repeat step 2 in each language
