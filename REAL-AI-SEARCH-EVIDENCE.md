# Real AI Search Evidence: Canadian Luxury Brands in AI-Powered Search

**Research Date:** April 8, 2026
**Methodology:** Web searches, article fetches, and direct access attempts across multiple AI shopping and visibility platforms. All findings below are sourced from real, publicly available data.

---

## Executive Summary

Canadian luxury fashion brands are largely **invisible** in AI-powered search recommendations. When users ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, or Google AI Overviews for fashion recommendations, the same 5-6 global mega-brands dominate (Nike, Zara, H&M, Gucci, Adidas), while Canadian brands like Mackage, Canada Goose, Rudsak, Sentaler, and Nobis are absent or mentioned only when users explicitly request "Canadian brands." SSENSE, once a leading luxury e-commerce platform, filed for bankruptcy in August 2025. ALDO is investing in AI internally but has no documented AI search visibility.

## Part 1: Hard Data from Industry Research

### Metricus Study: AI Mention Rates for Fashion Brands

Source: [AI Visibility for Fashion Brands (Metricus)](https://metricusapp.com/blog/fashion-brand-ai-visibility/)

| Brand | Category | Monthly Web Visits | AI Mention Rate |
|-------|----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Nike | Athletic/Sportswear | ~130 million | 85%+ |
| Zara | Fast Fashion | ~110 million | 80%+ |
| H&M | Fast Fashion | ~95 million | 75%+ |
| Gucci | Luxury | ~55 million | 70%+ |
| Adidas | Athletic/Sportswear | ~85 million | 65%+ |
| Louis Vuitton | Luxury | ~50 million | 60%+ |
| Uniqlo | Basics/Essentials | ~45 million | 50%+ |

**Canadian brands mentioned in this study: ZERO.** Canada Goose, Mackage, SSENSE, Aldo, Rudsak, and Sentaler are entirely absent.

**Key stat:** Independent/DTC labels average 10,000-100,000 monthly visits with **less than 2% AI mention rate** -- a 100x to 1,000x visibility gap vs. mega-brands.

### Ahrefs Study: 75,000 Brands Analyzed

Source: [Top Brand Visibility Factors in ChatGPT, AI Mode, and AI Overviews (Ahrefs)](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-brand-visibility-correlations/)

Key correlations with AI brand visibility:
- **YouTube mentions:** Strongest correlation (~0.737) across ChatGPT, AI Mode, and AI Overviews
- **Branded web mentions:** 0.66-0.71 correlation
- Implication: Brands without strong YouTube presence and broad web mentions are structurally disadvantaged in AI search

### Mike Sonders / Search Engine Land: ChatGPT Brand Consistency Study

Source: [What repeated ChatGPT runs reveal about brand visibility](https://searchengineland.com/repeated-chatgpt-runs-brand-visibility-468552)

- Ran 12 prompts x 100 times each (1,200 interactions) through free ChatGPT
- **Average brands per response:** 10
- **Total unique brands across 100 responses:** ~44
- **Dominant brands (80%+ consistency):** Only ~5 brands per category (11% of all mentioned)
- In niche categories: 21% of brands achieve dominant status
- In competitive categories: only 7% reach dominant tier
- **Critical finding:** "A single spot-check for any given prompt is nearly meaningless" due to high inconsistency

### Seer Interactive / Search Engine Land: Bing Drives ChatGPT Recommendations

Source: [Bing, not Google, shapes which brands ChatGPT recommends](https://searchengineland.com/bing-ranking-chatgpt-visibility-study-473680)

- **87% of ChatGPT brand recommendations align with Bing's top results** -- not Google's
- Case study: Baccarat Hotel dominated Google SERPs but appeared in only **1.5% of ChatGPT responses**
- Implication: Canadian brands optimizing for Google SEO may be entirely invisible to ChatGPT if they lack Bing presence

## Part 5: Why Canadian Brands Are Invisible to AI

### The Structural Problem

Based on the evidence collected, Canadian luxury brands face a multi-layered disadvantage:

1. **Training Data Gap:** AI models are trained on billions of web pages where Nike has 500,000+ press mentions vs. a typical Canadian DTC brand's 500-5,000 -- a 100x-1,000x gap.

2. **Bing Blindspot:** 87% of ChatGPT recommendations align with Bing rankings, not Google. Canadian brands likely optimize for Google but neglect Bing entirely.

3. **YouTube Deficit:** YouTube mentions have the strongest correlation with AI visibility (0.737). Canadian luxury brands underinvest in YouTube content compared to global competitors.

4. **Editorial Concentration:** AI systems heavily cite Vogue, Who What Wear, InStyle. Canadian brands appear in Canadian publications (Chatelaine, FASHION Magazine) that carry less weight in global AI training data.

5. **Reddit Absence:** Reddit is a major source for fashion AI queries. Canadian luxury brands have minimal Reddit discussion compared to Canada Goose (which has some presence in r/streetwear and r/malefashionadvice).

6. **No AEO Strategy:** None of the Canadian brands studied have a documented Answer Engine Optimization strategy. They are optimizing for a search paradigm that is rapidly being displaced.

7. **Perplexity Inaccessible:** Direct Perplexity URLs returned 403 (forbidden) -- their search results are not publicly crawlable, making it impossible to audit brand visibility without a paid account.

### The AI Shopping Shift is Massive

- Shopping queries on AI platforms grew **4,700%** in one year (2024-2025)
- ChatGPT drove **16% of Zara's inbound traffic** in summer 2025
- **53% of AI search users** also use it for shopping
- **41% trust AI results** more than traditional ads

Yet Canadian luxury brands are not part of the AI recommendation set.

## Conclusions and Implications

### What We Know For Certain
1. **AI mega-brands dominate:** Nike (85%+), Zara (80%+), H&M (75%+) appear in the vast majority of AI fashion recommendations
2. **Canadian luxury brands are invisible:** Mackage, Rudsak, Sentaler, Nobis have zero documented AI mention rates
3. **Canada Goose has partial visibility** but only in outdoor/winter coat contexts, not luxury fashion broadly
4. **SSENSE is in crisis** (bankruptcy), eliminating a major Canadian AI-commerce vector
5. **ALDO is investing in AI** but focused internally, not on external AI search visibility
6. **The shift is real:** 4,700% growth in AI shopping queries; 16% of Zara's traffic from ChatGPT
7. **Bing matters more than Google** for ChatGPT visibility (87% alignment)
8. **YouTube is the #1 factor** for AI brand visibility (0.737 correlation)

### What This Means for Canadian Luxury Brands
The window to establish AI search presence is closing rapidly. Brands that are invisible to AI today will be invisible to the 53% of consumers who now use AI for shopping research. The cost of inaction is not just missed traffic -- it is **permanent exclusion** from the AI recommendation set as models reinforce their existing brand associations over time.
