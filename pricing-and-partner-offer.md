## Part 1: Competitive Pricing Landscape

### Direct Competitors (AI Visibility Monitoring)

| Tool | Starter | Mid-Tier | Enterprise | Model |
|------|---------|----------|------------|-------|
| **Peec AI** | EUR 85/mo (50 prompts, 3 models) | EUR 205/mo (150 prompts, 2 projects) | EUR 425/mo (unlimited) | Per-prompt tracking |
| **Otterly.ai** | $29/mo (15 prompts) | $189/mo (100 prompts) | $489/mo (400 prompts) | Per-prompt + add-ons |
| **Semrush AI Visibility** | $99/mo (single toolkit) | $199/mo (bundled with SEO) | Custom | Add-on to SEO suite |
| **Profound** | $99/mo (ChatGPT only) | $399/mo (3 platforms, 100 prompts) | $2,000-5,000+/mo | Demo-gated, no self-serve |
| **Scrunch AI** | $250/mo (350 prompts) | Custom | Custom (annual discount) | Per-brand, per-month |
| **AthenaHQ** | $270/mo (3,500 credits) | $545/mo | $2,000+/mo | Credit-based usage |
| **Gauge** | $100/mo (100 daily prompts) | $599/mo (600 daily prompts) | Custom | Monitoring + content engine |

### Adjacent Competitors (Structured Data / Schema)

| Tool | Entry | Pro/Business | Model |
|------|-------|-------------|-------|
| **WordLift** | EUR 160/mo (agent plan) | EUR 799/mo (5 sites, knowledge graphs) | Schema automation + AI SEO |
| **Schema App** | Custom / enterprise | Custom (managed service) | Managed service, enterprise-only |

### Key Takeaways

1. **Price floor is ~$29/mo** (Otterly Lite) but that buys almost nothing (15 prompts).
2. **Meaningful plans cluster at $200-600/mo** for mid-market brands.
3. **Enterprise starts at $2,000/mo** and goes to $5,000+ for multi-brand operations.
4. **No one combines monitoring + remediation.** Every competitor only tracks visibility. None fix the underlying problems (schema, structured data, content optimization for LLMs). VisiMind's "AI Remediation Layer" is genuinely differentiated.
5. **Most competitors are monitor-only.** Gauge is closest to an end-to-end play (monitoring + content), but still does not do schema remediation or technical fixes.

## Part 2: VisiMind Pricing Tiers

### Positioning

VisiMind is not another AI visibility tracker. It is an **AI Remediation Layer**: it diagnoses how AI engines see your brand, then actually fixes the problems. This justifies a premium over pure monitoring tools, while undercutting enterprise solutions.

**Target customer:** Canadian luxury and premium brands ($1M-50M revenue) who cannot afford to be invisible in AI search but do not have in-house GEO expertise.

---

### Tier 1: Scan (Free)
*Lead magnet -- get brands into the funnel*

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| One-time AI Visibility Audit | Scan 1 brand across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews |
| Visibility Score | Simple 0-100 score with letter grade |
| Competitor Snapshot | Compare against 2 competitors |
| Problem Summary | High-level list of issues (schema gaps, citation gaps, sentiment risks) |
| Delivery | PDF report via email |
| Fix Kits | NOT included (upgrade prompt) |

**Purpose:** Generate leads. Every brand gets a free audit that shows them what is broken. The report ends with: "Here is what is wrong. Upgrade to Fix to get the remediation playbook."

**Justification:** Peec and Otterly offer free trials; Semrush offers a 14-day trial on bundled plans. A free audit is stickier than a time-limited trial because the brand keeps the report and sees their problems in writing.

---

### Tier 2: Fix (CAD $297/mo)
*Core product -- monitoring + remediation*

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Continuous AI Monitoring | Track 1 brand across 5 AI engines, 100 prompts, daily |
| AI Visibility Dashboard | Real-time scores, trends, sentiment, competitor comparison (up to 3) |
| Diagnose Reports | Monthly automated diagnosis of schema gaps, citation issues, content gaps |
| Fix Kits | Automated remediation playbooks: schema markup, content rewrites, structured data patches |
| Self-Consistency Mining | Golden Set probing to detect LLM contradictions about your brand |
| Email Alerts | Weekly summary + critical alerts (e.g., new competitor displaces you) |
| Support | Email support, onboarding call |

**Price Justification:**
- **CAD $297/mo = ~USD $215/mo.** This slots between Otterly Standard ($189) and Scrunch ($250), but includes remediation that neither offers.
- Canadian luxury brands think in CAD. Pricing in CAD feels more accessible than USD equivalents.
- $297 is below the psychological $300 barrier and signals premium-but-accessible.
- At this price, a brand spending $50K/year on digital marketing is paying <1% of budget for AI visibility + remediation.

---

### Tier 3: Transform (CAD $797/mo)
*Enterprise -- multi-brand, white-glove*

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Everything in Fix | All monitoring and remediation features |
| Multi-brand Support | Up to 5 brands / product lines |
| Expanded Monitoring | 500 prompts/day, all AI engines (7+), 10 competitors |
| Neuro-Symbolic EEE | Knowledge Graph integration, DPO constraints, External Environment Engineering |
| Priority Fix Kits | Expedited remediation with implementation guidance |
| Quarterly Strategy Call | 60-min call with Alejandro to review AI visibility strategy |
| API Access | Integrate VisiMind data into existing dashboards |
| Custom Reports | Branded reports for internal stakeholders |

**Price Justification:**
- **CAD $797/mo = ~USD $580/mo.** Competitive with Gauge Growth ($599) and AthenaHQ Growth ($545), but includes full remediation suite.
- Well below Profound Enterprise ($2,000-5,000+/mo), making it attractive for mid-market luxury brands that want enterprise features without enterprise pricing.
- The quarterly strategy call with the founder adds a personal touch that large competitors cannot match.

---

### Pricing Summary

| Tier | Price | Best For |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Scan** | Free | Any brand curious about AI visibility |
| **Fix** | CAD $297/mo | Single-brand luxury/premium businesses |
| **Transform** | CAD $797/mo | Multi-brand retailers, agencies, luxury groups |

**Annual discount:** 2 months free on annual plans (effectively ~17% off).

## Part 4: ROI Talking Points

### The Business Case for AI Search Visibility

Use these data-backed arguments when speaking with potential design partners or investors.

---

### 1. AI Search Traffic Is Exploding -- and It Converts Better

**The numbers:**
- AI-referred traffic to U.S. retail sites grew **4,700% year-over-year** as of mid-2025 (Adobe Analytics).
- During the 2025 holiday season, AI-driven traffic to retail jumped **693% YoY**, with **769% growth in November** alone.
- ChatGPT shopping traffic converts **31% higher** than non-branded organic search, based on a 12-month GA4 study of 94 seven- and eight-figure ecommerce brands (Visibility Labs / Search Engine Land).
- AI-driven **revenue per visit was up 254%** during the 2025 holiday season.
- Some studies show LLM referrals achieve an **18% conversion rate**, higher than paid shopping, SEO, and PPC.

**The talking point:**
*"AI search is not future -- it is the fastest-growing traffic source in ecommerce right now. And it converts better than Google organic. If your brand is invisible in AI answers, you are missing the highest-converting traffic channel available today."*

---

### 2. Zero-Click Means Zero Chance If You Are Not Cited

**The numbers:**
- **80% of consumers** now get answers from AI-generated results without clicking through to any website (zero-click).
- LLMs typically cite only **2-7 domains per response**, compared to Google's 10 organic results.
- Brands with the most web mentions appear **up to 10x more often** in AI-generated search results than their competitors (Ahrefs, 75,000-brand study).
- **41% of consumers** say they trust generative AI search results more than paid search ads (Adobe).

**The talking point:**
*"In traditional search, you had 10 spots on page one. In AI search, you have 2-7. If you are not one of those cited brands, 80% of consumers will never see you -- because they never click through to a website. There is no page two in AI search. There is only cited or invisible."*

---

### 3. Your Competitors Are Already Investing

**The numbers:**
- **Nearly one-third of digital marketing leaders** now prioritize Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) as their most critical performance channel for 2026.
- **97% of digital leaders** who have invested in GEO report a positive impact.
- **85% of US consumers** who use generative AI for shopping say it delivers a better experience than traditional search.
- **44% of users** who have tried AI-powered search say it is now their primary search method.

**The talking point:**
*"One-third of your competitors' marketing leaders are already prioritizing AI search optimization. 97% of them say it is working. Every month you wait, they are building the AI visibility moat that makes it harder for you to catch up."*

---

### 4. The Cost of Inaction for Luxury Brands Specifically

**The numbers:**
- The AI-enabled ecommerce market reached **$8.65 billion in 2025**, projected to hit **$22.60 billion by 2032**.
- AI-powered personalization increases revenue by **up to 40%** in high-traffic ecommerce.
- Luxury brands like Dior and Gucci are actively investing in AI-driven customer experiences.
- Business of Fashion's 2026 State of Fashion report identifies AI search as a key battleground for brand discovery.

**The talking point:**
*"Luxury brands compete on being known and desired. If ChatGPT recommends three skincare brands and yours is not one of them, that is not a traffic problem -- it is a brand positioning crisis. Your competitors are investing in AI visibility now. At CAD $297/month, VisiMind costs less than one Instagram influencer post, but it works 24/7 across every AI platform."*
