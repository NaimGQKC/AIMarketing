## KEY FINDINGS

### 1. Aldo Is Invisible in Non-Branded Searches
Out of 9 non-branded queries, Aldo appeared in ZERO. The brand is completely invisible for discovery-stage searches in both English and French.

### 2. Aldo Group Subsidiaries Have Marginal Presence
Call It Spring appeared in 2 out of 9 non-branded queries (sustainable-focused). Little Burgundy appeared in 1 (Montreal shoes). But these are not attributed to Aldo in any AI summary.

### 3. Aldo Is Invisible in Its Home City
For "best leather boots Montreal" and "meilleures chaussures Montreal," Aldo -- a company founded and headquartered in Montreal -- does not appear. Smaller local brands like Anfibio, Ateliers, and Maguire outrank it.

### 4. Sustainability Narrative Is Completely Missing
Despite Aldo's sustainability commitments, the brand appears in ZERO sustainability-focused queries (queries 1, 4, 7, 9, 12). Competitors like Allbirds, Vessi, Native Shoes, and Kamik dominate this space.

### 5. Branded Search Sentiment Is Problematic
When people DO search for Aldo specifically:
- English results show 2.5/5 stars on Yelp with "generally dissatisfied" customers
- AI summaries describe quality as "mediocre"
- Common complaints: sizing issues, durability problems, poor customer service
- French results are more positive but mostly from retail aggregators, not editorial content

### 6. French vs. English Sentiment Gap
French-language branded results ("Aldo bottes avis") are notably more positive than English ones ("Aldo shoes review"). This suggests a potential localization opportunity -- or a problem that French reviewers simply haven't yet surfaced.

### 7. AI-Generated Summaries Never Recommend Aldo
Across all 12 queries, AI-generated summaries/overviews mentioned Aldo ZERO times for non-branded queries. Even for branded queries, the English AI summary was predominantly negative.
