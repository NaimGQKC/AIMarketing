## KEY FINDINGS

### 1. mackage.com ranks #1 for only 1 out of 12 queries
The only query where mackage.com itself appears in the results is "luxury down jacket brands" (Query 4), where it holds position #1. For every other query -- including branded queries -- third-party sites control all rankings.

### 2. Complete absence from Montreal/local queries
For "best luxury winter coats Montreal" and "premium leather jackets Montreal," Mackage is entirely absent. This is especially notable because Mackage was FOUNDED in Montreal. Smaller local competitors (Gorski Montreal, Cuir Dimitri, LAMARQUE) rank instead.

### 3. Third parties control the Mackage brand narrative
Even for branded queries ("Mackage winter jacket review," "Mackage manteau avis"), mackage.com does not rank. Review blogs, Trustpilot, PurseForum, and third-party retailers (La Canadienne, Altitude Sports) control what consumers see about Mackage.

### 4. Critical French-language gap
Mackage is invisible for French Quebec queries. For "manteau hiver luxe femme Quebec," Audvik owns 2 of the top 3 spots. Mackage does not appear. Given Quebec's francophone market, this is a significant missed opportunity.

### 5. "Extreme cold" positioning ceded to competitors
Canada Goose, Pajar, Arctic Bay, and Quartz Co. own the "extreme cold" narrative. Mackage's -10C rating limits its presence here, but there is no content strategy to reframe Mackage's positioning for "stylish urban warmth" in these results.

### 6. Consistent competitor visibility
- **Canada Goose:** Dominates extreme cold queries, owns comparison narrative
- **Quartz Co.:** Appears in 4+ queries across English and French, strong Montreal/Quebec presence
- **Moose Knuckles:** Consistent presence in Canadian luxury outerwear queries
- **Nobis:** Homepage ranks directly for "best luxury outerwear brands Canada"
- **Audvik:** Dominates French Quebec women's winter coat queries

### 7. Comparison content positions Mackage as "less warm"
In "Mackage vs Canada Goose" results, the consistent narrative is: Mackage = style, Canada Goose = warmth. This may undermine Mackage's value proposition for Canadian consumers who prioritize warmth.
