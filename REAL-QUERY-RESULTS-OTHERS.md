## Key Strategic Findings

1. **Branded queries work; non-branded queries are the gap.** Rudsak, Dynamite, and Garage own their branded SERPs but are largely invisible for category/discovery queries.

2. **French-language SEO is a critical gap.** For "meilleurs manteaux cuir Montreal" (Query 4), Rudsak is completely absent despite being a Montreal leather brand. French queries are underserved across all target brands.

3. **Listicle articles dominate discovery queries.** Chatelaine, Highsnobiety, Good On You, Diary of a Toronto Girl, and Noovo Moi control the "best brands" narrative. Getting featured in these publications is essential.

5. **Sustainability is a blind spot.** None of the target brands appear for sustainable fashion queries, which is an increasingly important consumer segment.

6. **Aldo has zero visibility** across all 14 queries tested. Complete absence from fashion-related search results.

7. **SSENSE appears only once** (Query 10, luxury brands Montreal) despite being a globally recognized Montreal-based retailer.

8. **The "affordable fashion" space is unclaimed** by Dynamite/Garage despite their price positioning. H&M, Joe Fresh, and Ardene own this narrative.
