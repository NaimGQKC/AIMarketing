```

When ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Gemini ingest product catalogs, French-language content gets systematically degraded:

1. Accented characters (e, e, a, u) get compressed or dropped during tokenization. "Manteau en duvet d'oie" becomes garbled tokens that the model can't reliably reconstruct.

2. Quebec French vs. European French confusion. LLMs default to France-centric terminology. "Magasinage" (Quebec shopping) gets mapped to "faire du shopping" - meaning your localized content loses its specificity.

3. Bilingual mapping collapse. A jacket described as "800-fill power" in English and "facteur de gonflement 800" in French should be linked. In most LLMs, they're treated as completely separate (and sometimes contradictory) product attributes.
```

### Comment Engagement Rules
1. **Never pitch in the first comment.** Add genuine value first.
2. **Reply to replies.** If someone engages with your comment, continue the conversation.
3. **Name-drop data, not your product.** "Our research shows..." not "Our product does..."
4. **End with a conversational hook.** A question or an offer to share data.
5. **Comment within the first 2 hours** of the post going live for maximum visibility.
6. **Like and reply to the post author's response** to your comment to build the relationship.
