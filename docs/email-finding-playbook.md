## 3. Recommended Approach (Tomorrow Morning)

### Step 1: Pattern-guess the top 10 emails (15 min)
Using the patterns above, construct emails manually. For "Gregoire Baret" at Aldo Group: `gbaret@aldogroup.com`. For someone at SSENSE: `first.last@ssense.com`. This costs nothing and requires no account.

### Step 2: Verify with Hunter.io (10 min)
Sign up for Hunter.io free (takes 30 seconds with Google SSO). Use the **Email Verifier** (50 free verifications/month) to confirm each guessed email is deliverable. This avoids bounces that damage sender reputation.

### Step 3: Fill gaps with Apollo.io (15 min)
For any contacts where the pattern guess fails or you lack the person's name, sign up for Apollo free and search by company + title (e.g., "VP Digital Marketing" at "Mackage"). Apollo reveals the email directly.

**Total time: ~40 minutes for 10 verified emails. Zero cost.**

---

## 4. LinkedIn as Backup (Not Primary)

- **LinkedIn InMail** gets 10-15% response rates vs. 1-5% for cold email. However, free LinkedIn accounts cannot send InMail to non-connections.
- **LinkedIn connection request + message (300 chars)** is free and effective. Use this for C-suite contacts where email feels too cold.
- **Best practice:** Connect on LinkedIn AND email simultaneously. Multi-channel outreach increases response rates by 2-3x.

---

## 5. Google Dorking (Last Resort)

For hard-to-find contacts, try these Google searches:
```
"gregoire baret" email
"gregoire baret" "@aldogroup.com"
"kelly cochrane" "mackage" email
site:linkedin.com "@ssense.com" "marketing"
```
This occasionally surfaces emails from conference speaker bios, press releases, or public directories.
